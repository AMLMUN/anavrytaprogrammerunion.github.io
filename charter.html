AML MUN Charter

The shared standards that help every delegate, chair, staff member, and guest participate in a safe, respectful, and professional conference.

What it covers

A clear framework for conference life

The Charter sets out the responsibilities accepted by every AML MUN participant. It explains expected conduct and formal dress, protects participants from harassment and discrimination, provides emergency guidance, and defines attendance requirements and consequences.
