Organising Team

Guiding the conference behind the scenes and helping every part of AML MUN come together.

Head of the Organising Team

Natasha Panagiotou

My name is Natasha Panagiotou and I am studying mechanical engineering at NTUA. This year I have the utmost honour of serving as the Head of the Organising Team of the 5th AML MUN conference. I hope that the insight I have gained through my 20+ MUN participations will help make AML MUN a fun and fruitful conference. I wish everyone an enjoyable time!

Member of the Organising Team

Charalampos Gavouras

My name is Charalampos Gavouras and I am really excited and honoured to be a Member of the Organising Team of the 5th AML MUN conference. Ever since I joined MUN I have been thrilled by the experiences, the challenges and the amazing people I have met along the way. It is a place to grow, explore new perspectives and find your own voice. After some time away, AML MUN is finally back and we are working hard to create an unforgettable experience for everyone. I hope it will be a place where you feel inspired, confident, and connected to others who share the same curiosity and drive to better understand the world.
